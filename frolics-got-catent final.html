Judging criteria

Two rounds. One winner list.

Scoring runs the same way in both rounds — the audience and the judges each score independently, and the two scores are combined.

1

Perform

Every act gets a strict two-minute slot, in the order the coordinators set.

2

Get scored twice, at once

The audience scores you live by scanning a QR code at the door. Four judges score you from the panel. Both use a 1–10 scale in half-point steps.

3

The two averages combine

Your final score is the average of the audience's average and the judges' average, rounded to the nearest half point.

4

Top half advances

The top 50% of final scores move on to round two, where the whole process repeats.

5

Winners need agreement

You only make the winners' list if your rounded audience score and rounded judges score match exactly.

6

Top three take the prizes

Matching scores are ranked highest to lowest, and the top three are declared the winners.

Rules & regulations

House rules, kept short

The full rules and regulations are confirmed during script review. Here's what matters most on the night.

01

Keep it clean

No vulgar, obscene, hateful, or discriminatory content — in language, lyrics, or props.

02

Get it approved first

Scripts, audio, lyrics, and props must be submitted and approved before you go on. Unapproved changes can mean disqualification.

03

Stay in your two minutes

Coordinators set the running order and the clock. Both are final.

04

Play fair

Don't try to influence judges, manipulate audience scoring, or interfere with another act's performance.

05

Coordinators have the final word

Calls on rule violations, disqualifications, and disputes are final and binding.

06

No refunds on rejected scripts

If your script doesn't clear approval, your entry isn't refunded.
